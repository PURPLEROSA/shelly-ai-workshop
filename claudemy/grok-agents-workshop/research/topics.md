# Topics: personal AI agents from zero with Grok (beginner, no code)

Every reader files notes under these ids.

- T01 Chat vs agent: what makes an assistant an "agent" (acts on a schedule or trigger, uses tools, works without being asked each time)
- T02 Grok's building blocks map: custom instructions, Projects/Workspaces, Custom Agents, Skills, Memory, Connectors, Automations (formerly Tasks), Grok Bot. What each one is for
- T03 Custom instructions and Custom Agents: setup path, character limits, slots, how to write instructions
- T04 Projects and Workspaces: files and instructions as a business "brain", context separation
- T05 Memory: what Grok remembers, how to see/edit/turn it off, its limits
- T06 Skills: reusable instruction packages, built-in vs custom, how they connect to automations
- T07 Connectors: Gmail, Calendar, Drive and others, setup, permission levels (read vs write vs send)
- T08 Automations: schedules, email triggers, run now/test, plan tiers and limits
- T09 Grok Bot and autonomous computer agents: what it does, who has it, maturity
- T10 Writing in your own voice: style guides, samples, few-shot, iterating
- T11 Use case: business brain / leads and client replies
- T12 Use case: social media and trends to drafts (X real-time data, DeepSearch)
- T13 Use case: newsletter drafting
- T14 Use case: invoices, payment reminders, admin and inbox triage
- T15 Use case: family ops and the mental load
- T16 Agent teams / multi-step workflows (chaining, orchestrator, newsroom pattern)
- T17 Human in the loop: approval gates, drafts first, earning autonomy
- T18 Risks and limits: privacy and data training, reliability, hype vs reality, what not to give an agent
